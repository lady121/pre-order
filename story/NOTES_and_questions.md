# A Captive: Story Notes and Open Questions

## What the story is (as I read it)

A Beauty and the Beast retelling told from the side of **Bellina**, the fierce, older, dark-skinned sister who chases suitors off with a broom. **Belle** is her softer sister. **Gaston** starts as the boar she can't stand and slowly becomes the real love interest. **Phillip** is the sweet, younger first suitor who tells her she's beautiful. **Lefou** watches Gaston from the sidelines. Near the end she reveals she lived a **past life** in which she married Gaston and was smothered, and that he left her when "the princess" (Belle) needed saving.

## Questions that would change the draft

1. **Belle.** In the first scene she's alive. Later, a gossip says "if her sister were still alive." How does Belle die or leave, and when? Does she ever meet the Beast?
2. **Phillip.** What happens to him? I assumed he pulls away after the whorehouse incident and that's the "one chance" Bellina lost. Is that right? Does he get a send-off scene?
3. **The "captive."** Who is held captive and where? Is it Bellina (taken on Gaston's horse), Gaston by his own feelings, or someone else?
4. **Where does the horse ride in Chapter 8 go?**
5. **The past life.** Is it a memory she has had all along, or does it appear suddenly? Does anyone else remember? Is it Beauty and the Beast's "original" ending, with Gaston as the villain who gets killed?
6. **Is Gaston in this version a good man?** Your draft keeps both: he's possessive and says things like "a woman shouldn't dress so exposed," and he's also the one who is tender, who teaches her to shoot, and who sees her beauty.
7. **Tone and content.** Chapter 10 reads as adult. How explicit do you want to be? I've kept the draft at fade-to-black.
8. **Ending.** Do they stay together? What does "she used to be smothered, now she isn't" look like?

## Small continuity things I noticed

- The name is spelled **Bellina / Belinna / Belina** in different places. I used **Bellina**.
- **Maria** in the stage scene is never introduced. Who is she?
- **Daisy the pig** is a good recurring gag.
- The cottage and "family home" matter to the marriage plot (she needs a husband to keep it), but this is never stated clearly.
